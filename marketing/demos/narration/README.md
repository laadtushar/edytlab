# Narration for the DJ demos: none is current

The voice-over for the three DJ demos on the website was made for the earlier recordings and moved, with its script, voice tracks, timing and checks, to [`../../archive/demos-narration/2026-10-26-old-recordings/`](../../archive/README.md) when the demos were re-recorded on 2026-10-26: the lines said things the new takes do not (a tempo and key to ask for, BPM numbers, a 1:21 export), and the narrated mp4s showed the replaced footage. The earlier beatmatch narration is in `../../archive/demos-narration/2026-10-24-f-major-line/`.

To narrate the new recordings: write lines from `../../reels/tools/spec.py` (`LINES`, one per caption moment, no numbers or readings) and `../../reels/demo-*-cues.json` (the timing windows), obey the "Evergreen media rules" in [`../README.md`](../README.md), generate on a paid ElevenLabs plan (the free plan's licence does not allow commercial use), and mix with `../../reels/add-narration.sh`. The website's demos carry no voice.
