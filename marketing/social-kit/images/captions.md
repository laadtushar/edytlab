# Captions for the social images

One caption per platform for each of the six themes. Each goes with the image
of that theme at the platform's size (see [README.md](README.md) for which file).
Nothing here posts anywhere by itself.

How they are written: each caption names the moment its image shows, tells a
line or two of story, then gives the link. A caption says one thing and carries
at most one limit, said lightly. Themes 1 to 5 link a video or the privacy page,
so they skip the unsigned installers; theme 6 sends people to the code and the
downloads, so it says so. Hashtags are three at most. Nothing here is emoji.

Facts used, all true of the released app: free and open source (MIT); macOS,
Windows and Linux; the installers are unsigned; audio is edited on your
computer and never uploaded, only the chat goes to the provider you choose;
Ollama runs locally with no key; every edit is a node in a history graph. No
caption claims anything that has not shipped, and none carries a version number
or a user count. The `edytlab.com/videos/<slug>` pages are the site's own watch
pages. The frames of themes 1, 5 and 6 come from the v0.7.0 video
(`marketing/promo-v0.7.0/out/`), which the site stopped serving after v0.9.1, so
theme 1 links the launch video (a typed request becoming tool calls) and theme 5
the videos page.

Limits checked by script when this file was written: X under 280 characters
(a link counts as 23), Threads and Bluesky under 300, YouTube titles under 70.
Re-count if you edit.
